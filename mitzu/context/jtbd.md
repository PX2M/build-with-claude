# Jobs to be done

> Step 2 of the chain. The ICP says who. This file says what they are trying to get done, so
> the messaging answers a job instead of listing features.
> Format: When (the situation), I want to (the motivation), so I can (the outcome).
> Every job is grounded in a public testimonial or page on mitzu.io. Sources at the bottom.

## Job 1. Answer my own product question (the product person)
- **When** I have a product question, like where users drop off in onboarding or who comes back
  after week one,
- **I want to** ask it in plain language and get the funnel or retention chart myself,
- **so I can** decide this week, instead of waiting days in the data team's queue.
- **The proof it is real:** "We went from waiting days for reports to getting instant answers."
  (CMO, Munch). "The onboarding funnel improved by 30%, and ad hoc reporting is 50% faster."
  (Director of Product Management, Colossyan)
- **What Mitzu offers:** "Ask funnel, retention, and cohort questions in plain language."
  (mitzu.io homepage)

## Job 2. Stop being the bottleneck, without losing control of the numbers (the data lead)
- **When** product and marketing keep sending me ad hoc SQL requests,
- **I want** them to answer their own questions on the tables I already govern,
- **so I can** get my team's time back and know the numbers they see match mine.
- **The proof it is real:** "Our engineering team no longer gets flooded with ad-hoc SQL
  requests - everyone can self-serve with confidence." (CTO, Rythm). "Our business teams see
  the same interface, but under the hood, everything is more powerful and easier to maintain."
  (Data Architect, Suunto)
- **What Mitzu offers:** "deterministic, reviewable SQL your team can trust" on a semantic layer
  that "stays yours". (mitzu.io homepage)

## Job 3. Keep one copy of the data, and stop paying for the second (the data lead, the budget owner)
- **When** we pay a third-party tool to store a copy of events we already keep in the warehouse,
- **I want** product analytics to run on the warehouse itself,
- **so I can** stop the cost growing with every event, and keep the data first party.
- **The proof it is real:** "Before Mitzu, we struggled with high costs and incomplete data."
  (Data Lead, Shapr3D). "Avoids redundant data storage ... at half the cost of standard product
  analytics tools." (Head of Data Engineering, Khatabook). "A godsend for folks who have to keep
  all the data first party." (Senior Product Manager, Transfr)
- **What Mitzu offers:** "We don't ingest, store, or move your data." (mitzu.io/privacy-security).
  Per-editor-seat pricing, unlimited events. (mitzu.io/pricing)

## What they fire to hire Mitzu
- A third-party analytics tool that holds its own copy of the events. "Removing the uncertainty
  of third-party tools." (Lead Data Analyst, Prezi)
- The data team's ticket queue, for everyday product questions.
