# Who it is for, and who it is not

## The reader of every output
A rep, ninety seconds before a demo. Skimmable. Objection, why it lands, what to say back, one source.

## ICP
- VP Product, Head of Growth, Director of Product Analytics. Head of Data often signs.
- B2B SaaS, already on Snowflake, BigQuery or Databricks, data team of three or more.
- Trigger: the product tool and the warehouse disagree, three days before a board meeting.

## Anti-ICP (these do more work than the ICP)
- No warehouse. Disqualify.
- Marketing owns analytics, wants dashboards not SQL. Amplitude fits better. Say so.
- Too small to have a data team. Not yet.
