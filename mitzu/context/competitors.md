# Who to watch

Two named companies. Not "the market".

## Amplitude
- Pricing: amplitude.com/pricing
- Changelog: amplitude.com/changelog
- What to flag: event-volume tiers, sampling or data-retention limits, any category widening on the homepage.

## Mixpanel
- Pricing: mixpanel.com/pricing
- Changelog: mixpanel.com/changelog
- What to flag: event pricing changes, anything shipped on sampled data, any warehouse-native language (that is them moving onto our wedge).

## The wedge to defend
Warehouse-native. Anything that touches cost at scale, sampling, or moving data out of the warehouse is a signal. A feature release that does not touch one of those three is noise.
