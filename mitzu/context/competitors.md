# Who to watch

Two named companies. Not "the market".

## Amplitude
- Pricing: amplitude.com/pricing
- Changelog: amplitude.com/releases
- What to flag: event-volume tiers, free-plan limits, anything that makes moving data INTO Amplitude easier, any category widening on the homepage.

## Mixpanel
- Pricing: mixpanel.com/pricing
- Changelog: docs.mixpanel.com/changelogs
- What to flag: event pricing changes, warehouse connector changes, any warehouse-native language (that is them moving onto our wedge).

## The wedge to defend
Warehouse-native. Anything that touches cost at scale, moving data out of the warehouse, or where the AI reads from (their copy or your warehouse) is a signal. A feature release that does not touch one of those three is noise.
