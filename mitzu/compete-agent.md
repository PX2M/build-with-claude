# compete-agent.md

Watch:   amplitude.com, mixpanel.com, their changelogs, their pricing pages
Flag:    pricing changes, data-movement moves (imports, connectors, warehouse language), positioning shifts, funding
Ignore:  feature releases that do not touch our wedge (cost at scale, data movement, where the AI reads)
Output:  one battle card covering the four objections in context/icp.md. Each: why it lands, what a rep says back,
         proof with a source URL. Save to outputs/battle-card.md. Signals you kept and dropped go to outputs/signals.md.
Rule:    every claim needs a source. No source, no card.
Rule:    name where we LOSE. A card with no losing case gets ignored.
Rule:    the reader is a rep with ninety seconds. Skimmable, or it is not done.
