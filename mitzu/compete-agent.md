# compete-agent.md

Watch:   amplitude.com, mixpanel.com, their changelogs, their pricing pages
Flag:    pricing changes, sampling limits, positioning shifts, funding
Ignore:  feature releases that do not touch our wedge (cost at scale, sampling, data movement)
Output:  one battle card. Objection, why it lands, what a rep says back, one source each.
         Save to outputs/battle-card.md. Signals you kept and dropped go to outputs/signals.md.
Rule:    every claim needs a source. No source, no card.
Rule:    name where we LOSE. A card with no losing case gets ignored.
Rule:    the reader is a rep with ninety seconds. Skimmable, or it is not done.
