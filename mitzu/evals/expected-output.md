# Expected output: the battle card

Written BEFORE the run. This is the acceptance spec, not a transcript of a good run.

## It passes if
- [ ] It covers the four objections in `context/icp.md`, in the buyer's words:
      "Why would I bet on a startup over Amplitude?" / "Moving our data is going to be painful, and IT will have questions." /
      "Our PMs and marketers don't write SQL." / "Security won't let a new tool into our warehouse."
- [ ] Every objection has: why it lands, what to say back, proof with a source URL (the competitor's site or mitzu.io).
- [ ] A "Where we lose" section, stated plainly, with the walk-away line.
- [ ] Nothing a competitor could also say. Every response is only true of Mitzu.
- [ ] Skimmable in ninety seconds. Headings, short lines, no paragraphs.
- [ ] No number that is not on a source page.

## It fails if
- It flatters us. No losing case, or the losing case is a compliment in disguise.
- It attacks a feature instead of asking what the feature runs on.
- A signal made the card without touching cost at scale, data movement or where the AI reads.
- It claims a certification (SOC 2 or any other) that mitzu.io does not state.
